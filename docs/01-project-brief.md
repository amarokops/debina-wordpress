# Dębina — Project Brief

**Project:** Dębina Carpentry Workshop Website\
**Project type:** Self-initiated portfolio project\
**Status:** Planning\
**Website:** debina.accelerock.pl

## 1. Project Overview

Dębina is a fictional small carpentry workshop based in the Płock area, Poland. The company specializes in custom-made furniture, kitchens, wardrobes, and wooden interior fittings.

The purpose of this project is to design and implement a professional WordPress website that helps a local service business establish an online presence, showcase its craftsmanship, and generate customer inquiries.

The project simulates a real client engagement, from initial business requirements to implementation, testing, and deployment.

## 2. Business Problem

The workshop currently relies primarily on word-of-mouth recommendations and lacks a dedicated online presence.

Potential customers have no convenient way to explore previous projects, understand the company's services, or submit detailed requests for quotations.

The website should address these limitations by providing a clear, trustworthy, and accessible digital point of contact.

## 3. Business Objectives

- Establish a professional and credible online presence.
- Present the workshop's services and completed projects.
- Encourage potential customers to request quotations.
- Make contact information easily accessible on all devices.
- Support local discoverability through basic SEO practices.

## 4. Target Audience

The primary audience consists of homeowners and apartment owners in Płock and surrounding areas who are looking for custom-made furniture or carpentry services.

These visitors typically want to evaluate the quality of previous work, understand the services offered, and contact the workshop for an individual quotation.

## 5. Project Scope

The website will include:

- Homepage with a clear value proposition and calls to action.
- Services overview and dedicated service pages.
- Portfolio section with individual project case studies.
- About page introducing the workshop.
- Contact page with an inquiry form.
- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- Basic search engine optimization, performance optimization, and security measures.

The project does not include online payments, product ordering, customer accounts, or a booking system.

## 6. Technology Stack

- WordPress CMS
- Blocksy Free theme
- Gutenberg block editor
- Stackable Free
- Additional free plugins where functionally necessary

## 7. Success Criteria

The completed website should:

- Provide clear navigation to services, portfolio, and contact information.
- Allow visitors to submit an inquiry through a functional contact form in a controlled test environment.
- Display correctly across common screen sizes.
- Meet basic accessibility, performance, and SEO requirements.
- Allow non-technical administrators to update service information and add portfolio projects using reusable content patterns.

## 8. Project Deliverables

- Functional demonstration website.
- Documented information architecture and design system.
- Reusable page and portfolio layouts.
- Technical implementation notes.
- Quality assurance checklist and test results.
- Final project case study.

## 9. Project Assumptions

Dębina is a fictional business created for demonstration purposes. Business details, project descriptions, and example content are illustrative.

The website is not intended to represent a real operating company or collect genuine commercial inquiries.

## 10. Project Constraints

- Use free WordPress themes and plugins wherever practical.
- Avoid unnecessary plugin dependencies.
- Keep the website maintainable by a non-technical administrator.
- Prioritize performance, mobile usability, and straightforward content management.
